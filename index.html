At ByteDance Seed, I focus on post-training for Seedream foundation models. Previously, I was a core contributor to distillation and acceleration for Qwen-Image-2.0 and Qwen-Image-3.0 at Alibaba.

I am pursuing my Ph.D. at CityU Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Kede Ma. My broader interests include generative world models and data valuation.
