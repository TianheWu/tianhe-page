About Me

Hi, I'm Tianhe Wu, a Ph.D. student at the City University of Hong Kong (CityU), where I am supervised by Prof. Kede Ma. My current research focuses on Generative AI (GenAI), with a particular interest in post-training techniques for LLMs and VLMs.

Before joining CityU, I completed my M.Sc. degree (2022-2025) at Tsinghua University, under the supervision of Prof. Yujiu Yang. I received my B.Eng. degree (2018-2022) from the Beijing University of Technology.
